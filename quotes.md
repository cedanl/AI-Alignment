## The Promise of AI

> "We're making this analogy that AI is the new electricity. Electricity
> transformed industries: agriculture, transportation, communication,
> manufacturing."
>
> — **[Andrew Ng](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/andrew-ng-quotes)**, co-founder of Google Brain and Coursera

> "Some people call this artificial intelligence, but the reality is this
> technology will enhance us. So instead of artificial intelligence, I think
> we'll augment our intelligence."
>
> — **[Ginni Rometty](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/ginni-rometty-quotes)**, former CEO of IBM

> "I'd rather see artificial intelligence than no intelligence."
>
> — **[Michael Crichton](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/michael-crichton-quotes)**, author

> "We're living in an age where new technology offers gigantic upsides -
> artificial intelligence has the potential to diagnose cancer, catch serial
> killers and reduce prison populations."
>
> — **[Hannah Fry](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/hannah-fry-quotes)**, mathematician and author

> "The promise of artificial intelligence and computer science generally
> vastly outweighs the impact it could have on some jobs in the same way
> that, while the invention of the airplane negatively affected the railroad
> industry, it opened a much wider door to human progress."
>
> — **[Paul Allen](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/paul-allen-quotes)**, co-founder of Microsoft

> "I believe this artificial intelligence is going to be our partner. If we
> misuse it, it will be a risk. If we use it right, it can be our partner."
>
> — **[Masayoshi Son](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/masayoshi-son-quotes)**, founder of SoftBank

> "Artificial intelligence will reach human levels by around 2029. Follow
> that out further to, say, 2045, we will have multiplied the intelligence,
> the human biological machine intelligence of our civilization a
> billion-fold."
>
> — **[Ray Kurzweil](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/ray-kurzweil-quotes)**, inventor and futurist

> "Those years should be intellectually the most creative and exciting in the
> history of mankind."
>
> — **[J. C. R. Licklider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._C._R._Licklider)**, computer scientist, on man-computer symbiosis

## Risks & Alignment

> "With artificial intelligence, we are summoning the demon. You know all
> those stories where there's the guy with the pentagram and the holy water,
> and he's like, yeah, he's sure he can control the demon? Doesn't work out."
>
> — **[Elon Musk](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/elon-musk-quotes)**, CEO of Tesla and SpaceX

> "It [AI] would take off on its own and redesign itself at an ever
> increasing rate. Humans, who are limited by slow biological evolution,
> couldn't compete and would be superseded."
>
> — **[Stephen Hawking](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/stephen-hawking-quotes)**, physicist

> "By far, the greatest danger of Artificial Intelligence is that people
> conclude too early that they understand it."
>
> — **[Eliezer Yudkowsky](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/eliezer-yudkowsky-quotes)**, AI alignment researcher

> "By their very nature, heuristic shortcuts will produce biases, and that
> is true for both humans and artificial intelligence, but the heuristics
> of AI are not necessarily the human ones."
>
> — **[Daniel Kahneman](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/daniel-kahneman-quotes)**, Nobel laureate in economics

> "Like all technologies before it, artificial intelligence will reflect the
> values of its creators. So inclusivity matters - from who designs it to who
> sits on the company boards and which ethical perspectives are included."
>
> — **[Kate Crawford](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/kate-crawford-quotes)**, AI researcher and author

> "We must address, individually and collectively, moral and ethical issues
> raised by cutting-edge research in artificial intelligence and
> biotechnology, which will enable significant life extension, designer
> babies, and memory extraction."
>
> — **[Klaus Schwab](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/klaus-schwab-quotes)**, founder of the World Economic Forum

> "I do think we need to at least register artificial intelligence units to
> be able to monitor and control them. Because we want to control them and
> not vice versa."
>
> — **[Jens Martin Skibsted](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/jens-martin-skibsted-quotes)**, designer and author

> "I don't think that an artificially intelligent system that has superhuman
> intelligence will be violent. I do think that it will disrupt our culture."
>
> — **[Gray Scott](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/gray-scott-quotes)**, futurist

## What AI Is

> "I believe that at the end of the century the use of words and general
> educated opinion will have altered so much that one will be able to speak
> of machines thinking without expecting to be contradicted."
>
> — **[Alan Turing](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/alan-turing-quotes)**, mathematician and computer scientist

> "I often tell my students not to be misled by the name 'artificial
> intelligence' - there is nothing artificial about it. AI is made by
> humans, intended to behave by humans, and, ultimately, to impact humans'
> lives and human society."
>
> — **[Fei-Fei Li](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/fei-fei-li-quotes)**, professor at Stanford University

> "Nobody phrases it this way, but I think that artificial intelligence is
> almost a humanities discipline. It's really an attempt to understand human
> intelligence and human cognition."
>
> — **[Sebastian Thrun](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/sebastian-thrun-quotes)**, founder of Udacity

> "The sad thing about artificial intelligence is that it lacks artifice and
> therefore intelligence."
>
> — **[Jean Baudrillard](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/jean-baudrillard-quotes)**, philosopher

> "The key to artificial intelligence has always been the representation."
>
> — **[Jeff Hawkins](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/jeff-hawkins-quotes)**, co-founder of Numenta

> "The techniques of artificial intelligence are to the mind what
> bureaucracy is to human social interaction."
>
> — **[Terry Winograd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_Winograd)**, professor at Stanford University

> "A lot of what people are calling 'artificial intelligence' is really data
> analytics - in other words, business as usual. If the hype leaves you
> asking 'What is A.I., really?,' don't worry, you're not alone."
>
> — **[Om Malik](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/om-malik-quotes)**, technology writer

> "Forget artificial intelligence - in the brave new world of big data,
> it's artificial idiocy we should be looking out for."
>
> — **[Tom Chatfield](https://www.brainyquote.com/authors/tom-chatfield-quotes)**, author and commentator
