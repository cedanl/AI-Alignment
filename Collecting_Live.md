# Collecting Live Intelligence

# Many Sources

There are numerous sources of live intelligence. [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai) is a prime example and one well worth exploring. The challenge lies in identifying a set of similar sources whose measured outputs do not overlap too extensively, and which are also updated on a regular basis. Such sources do exist, but coherently maintaining the aggregated and cleaned accumulation of results requires considerable effort.
